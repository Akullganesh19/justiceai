## 🌌 Emergent Feature Added

**What:** Added a "Draft History" section to the DocumentsPage that persists generated procedural documents locally.
**Derivation:** Pattern 3: Actions Without Memory. Users generate documents but the app previously lost them upon navigation. This adds local persistence for those generated drafts.
**Why it was missing:** Likely an oversight during the initial V1 document generation build, where the focus was entirely on the generation logic rather than user retention/reusability.
**User story:** As a user, I can now revisit, review, and re-export previously generated legal documents without having to refill the entire form.
**Data used:** Selected Template ID, Form Data, Generated Text, and Timestamp.
**Discovery:** Users will see their previous drafts at the bottom of the main Documents selection page.
**Next:** "Pattern 4: Events Without Notification" -> Case Tracker could proactively remind users based on timeline expectations.
