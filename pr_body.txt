## 🧠 Cross-System Intelligence Added

**What:** Implemented an Event Bridge to connect the isolated AI Chat and Case Tracker systems.
**Systems:** AI Chat System ↔ Case Tracker System.
**Intelligence emerged:** Legal strategy timelines generated during chat consultations are now automatically provisioned as trackable milestone cases in the Case Tracker. This unlocks actionable tracking for abstract chat advice.
**Data flow:** When an AI analysis completes in `ChatPage`, a `justice-ai-analysis-complete` custom event is emitted carrying the timeline payload. The centralized `eventBridge` intercepts this, normalizes the data, and securely persists it to the Case Tracker's `localStorage` namespace, avoiding duplicates.
**Coupling:** Event Bridge pattern. `src/main.jsx` initializes the listener globally. Neither system directly imports or acknowledges the other, ensuring extremely loose coupling.
