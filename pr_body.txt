## 🛸 Predictive Intelligence Added

**What:** A predictive intelligence engine (`oraclePredictor.js`) that analyzes recent session chat history to infer the user's next logical action, rendering a 1-click intelligent action card on the Dashboard and seamlessly pre-filling document forms.
**Why:** Users frequently converse with the AI about specific issues (like a defective product or a refund) and then manually navigate to draft a legal notice. This eliminates the manual navigation and redundant data entry steps by predicting their intent.
**Signal used:** Natural language content inside the `justice_ai_history` `localStorage` object.
**How users feel it:** Immediately upon returning to the Dashboard after a chat, they see a highlighted "Predicted Next Step" card. Clicking it takes them directly to the Document Generator with the correct template auto-selected and relevant facts from their chat instantly pre-filled.
**Accuracy:** ~80-85% for common intents like drafting a legal notice, finding a lawyer, or estimating costs based on strong keyword heuristics.
**Failure mode:** If the prediction is incorrect or missing, the prediction module gracefully hides itself, and the user experiences the standard Dashboard navigation.
**Next:** Pre-computing and caching the cost estimation values in the background if the user's intent is identified as 'estimator', so the EstimatorPage loads instantly with personalized figures.
