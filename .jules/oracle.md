## 2024-05-24 — Predictive Navigation Mesh
**Product understood as:** An AI-Powered Legal Co-pilot for Indian Citizens with multiple tools (chat, estimator, forms, docs).
**Prediction invented:** Behavioral route prefetching based on Markov chain navigation graph. It learns user-specific workflows in real time.
**Data used:** Sequential transitions of routes visited within a user's session, stored in localStorage as `oracle_nav_graph`.
**Impact:** Users will experience zero perceived latency for predictable flows (e.g., typically visiting /dashboard after /chat), as the next expected chunk is prefetched before they click.
**Next opportunity:** Predicting form auto-fills in the Document Generator or Case Tracker based on previously supplied details.
