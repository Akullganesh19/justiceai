## 2024-05-24 — Intelligent Identity Defaults
**Product understood as:** An AI-powered legal co-pilot that helps Indian citizens draft statutory notices and procedural applications.
**Prediction invented:** Auto-filling of user identity details (name, address, phone) across different legal document templates.
**Data used:** Form submissions in the Document Generator. When a user generates a document, their identity details are saved to localStorage (`justice_ai_oracle_identity`).
**Impact:** Users don't have to re-type their own name, address, and phone number every time they generate a new legal document. The app "remembers" them across templates.
**Next opportunity:** Prefetching document templates or predicting which template a user will need based on their chat history or previous documents generated.
