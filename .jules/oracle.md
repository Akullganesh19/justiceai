## 2024-05-24 — Predictive Form Pre-filling
**Product understood as:** An AI legal co-pilot that helps users discuss cases and draft statutory documents.
**Prediction invented:** Predictive document form pre-filling. When users open a drafting template after chatting, the form anticipates their needs by auto-populating narrative fields using the context from their most recent case consultation.
**Data used:** The `justice_ai_history` from `localStorage`, specifically the latest case's analysis summary or user message.
**Impact:** Eliminates redundant typing. Users feel the app has continuous memory from consultation to drafting, creating a magical "it already knows my case" experience.
**Next opportunity:** Predictive next-document recommendations based on case outcome or current stage.
