## 2024-05-24 — Predictive Route Prefetching

**Product understood as:** A React-based SPA legal co-pilot for Indian citizens, featuring multi-page tools (chat, estimators, docs).
**Prediction invented:** Implemented a hover-based route chunk prefetching system using a centralized map.
**Data used:** User mouse movement over `<a>` tags (hover intent signal).
**Impact:** Perceived loading times for subsequent pages will drop from hundreds of milliseconds to near-zero as chunks are preloaded in the background before the user clicks.
**Next opportunity:** Behavior-based pre-fetching, such as pre-loading the specific case tracker chunk automatically after a legal consultation session ends.
