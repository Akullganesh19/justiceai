## 2024-05-24 — Saved Documents
**Product understood as:** A local AI legal co-pilot that helps Indian citizens understand their rights and generate procedural legal documents.
**Derivation reasoning:** Users can generate procedural documents (like legal notices and complaints) through a multi-step form. However, once a document is generated, it vanishes when the page is closed or reset. Since legal matters take time and documents are frequently referenced, copied, or downloaded later, users obviously need a persistent library of their generated documents.
**Feature built:** Added a "Saved Documents" section to the Documents page that persists generated documents in localStorage, allowing users to access, copy, and export past drafts across sessions.
**User impact:** Users can now build a persistent library of their generated legal documents, referencing and exporting them at any time without re-typing their details.
**Next logical feature:** Document editing/refining capability to adjust generated drafts directly within the platform.
