## 2024-05-15 — Document Vault
**Product understood as:** An AI legal assistant that helps citizens understand rights and draft procedural documents.
**Derivation reasoning:** This product has a Document Generator where users input significant incident data to generate drafts. Users do this repeatedly to prepare legal actions. Therefore users obviously need a Document Vault to save past drafts — because users need to reference or re-download these documents later, and losing a drafted legal document on refresh is a terrible experience. It didn't exist because the generator was built as a stateless utility.
**Feature built:** A persistent Document Vault in the Documents page that automatically saves generated drafts to local storage, allowing one-click retrieval and management.
**User impact:** Users no longer lose their drafted documents and can maintain a history of their procedural legal paperwork.
**Next logical feature:** "Convert Chat to Tracked Case" to connect AI consultation directly to execution tracking.
