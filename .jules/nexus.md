## 2024-05-18 — Document Vault (Saved Drafts)\n**Product understood as:** An AI legal assistant that allows users to chat for advice, read up on rights, and importantly, generate structural legal documents like FIRs, RTI requests, and Notices.\n**Derivation reasoning:** This product has [legal document templates and generated outputs]. Users do [fill out complex multi-step forms to generate documents]. Therefore users obviously need [a way to save their drafted documents within the app for later reference/editing] — because [legal documents take time to perfect, users might need to stop and come back, or refer to an old drafted notice for a new case]. It doesn't exist because [it was likely overlooked in favor of immediate PDF export]. I'm building it because [it adds crucial stateful memory to the document generation flow, turning it from a stateless utility into a workspace].\n**Feature built:** A Document Vault that saves generated drafts to `localStorage` (`justice_ai_documents`), viewable on the `DocumentsPage`, with the ability to delete or re-view saved drafts.\n**User impact:** Users can now generate a document, and it will be saved in their browser. They can come back to the Documents page to see their history of generated documents.\n**Next logical feature:** Ability to edit a saved draft by loading it back into the FormWizard.
