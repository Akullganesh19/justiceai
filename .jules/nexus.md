## 2024-05-24 — Document Vault
**Product understood as:** An AI-powered legal co-pilot that helps Indian citizens understand rights and draft procedural documents.
**Derivation reasoning:** The product has a Document Generator that creates detailed legal templates (Statutory Notices, RTIs, etc.). Users repeatedly generate and customize these complex documents (Pattern 3: Actions Without Memory). However, once generated, the document is only available to copy or download as PDF. If the user leaves the page or refreshes, their drafted document is lost forever. Therefore, users obviously need a way to save and retrieve their drafted documents because drafting legal documents is an iterative process that requires saving state for future reference or modification. It doesn't exist because the initial implementation prioritized the generation capability over client-side persistence. I'm building it because it significantly improves the utility of the Document Generator, allowing users to build a personal repository of their legal drafts without fear of data loss.
**Feature built:** A Document Vault within the Document Generator page that securely saves generated drafts to local storage, complete with a tabbed interface to view, copy, export, and delete saved documents.
**User impact:** Users can now safely save their generated legal drafts directly within the browser, retrieve them later, and manage their document history.
**Next logical feature:** Pattern 1 (Data Without Insight): A unified dashboard or timeline view that aggregates activity across the AI Chat, Case Tracker, and Document Vault into a single coherent "Legal Journey" timeline.
