## 2024-05-24 — Export AI Timeline to Case Tracker
**Product understood as:** An AI legal copilot that helps Indian citizens draft documents and understand their case strategy and timeline.
**Derivation reasoning:** The app generates a customized legal timeline in the AI chat. However, the case tracker forces users to use generic manual templates. Connecting them allows users to instantly track AI-generated custom timelines, making the feature significantly more useful.
**Feature built:** Added an "Export to Case Tracker" button to the AI Timeline component. It saves the AI-generated timeline as a new active case in the Case Tracker's localStorage and navigates the user there.
**User impact:** Users can now seamlessly convert their AI-generated case strategy into an actionable, trackable dashboard.
**Next logical feature:** Add push notifications or reminders when a case tracker step is marked as incomplete and past due.
