## 2025-02-14 — Secure Document Vault
**Product understood as:** A comprehensive legal co-pilot that helps citizens understand laws, track cases, and generate legal documents (notices, complaints) securely.
**Derivation reasoning:** The product allows generating crucial legal documents in "Draft Documents", but leaves no memory of them. Users generate a document, download it, and if they leave the page, it's gone forever. They repeatedly generate complex documents without the app remembering them. Therefore they obviously need a Secure Document Vault — because managing legal matters requires keeping records over time. It doesn't exist because the generator was likely built as a stateless utility. I'm building it because it provides a foundational necessity for any legal assistant: a persistent record of created artifacts.
**Feature built:** Secure Document Vault integrated directly into the Procedural Drafting (Documents) page. Users can save drafts, view their vault, recall previous drafts, and delete them.
**User impact:** Users no longer lose their carefully crafted legal notices if they close the tab. They have a secure, local-first repository of their generated legal documents that persists across sessions.
**Next logical feature:** Since users can save generated drafts and track case progress, the next logical step is connecting the two: attaching vaulted documents directly to milestones in the Case Tracker.
