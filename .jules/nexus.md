## 2025-05-24 — Document Vault
**Product understood as:** An AI legal co-pilot for citizens, featuring document generation, chat, and trackers.
**Derivation reasoning:** Users repeatedly generate complex legal documents, but the app does not remember them. The drafts are lost on tab close. Saving them locally fills a critical "Actions Without Memory" gap.
**Feature built:** Local storage-backed Document Vault that saves generated drafts automatically and allows users to revisit, copy, download, or delete them.
**User impact:** Users can now securely retrieve previous drafts without needing to re-enter all form data.
**Next logical feature:** User profiles or cross-device syncing of local storage.
