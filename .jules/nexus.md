## 2024-05-19 — Document Vault
**Product understood as:** An AI legal assistant that helps citizens understand rights and draft procedural documents.
**Derivation reasoning:** The app generates complex legal documents through a wizard. Users generate these but have no way to save, retrieve, or revisit them later without regenerating. Therefore, users obviously need a vault to store generated documents locally.
**Feature built:** Added a "Save to Vault" action on the document generation preview and a Document Vault grid on the main documents page to view and delete saved templates.
**User impact:** Users can now persist their drafted documents securely in their local browser storage for later reference or reuse without re-entering form data.
**Next logical feature:** Generating a PDF is a one-way street. Users may need to edit specific sections of the generated document manually before export, suggesting an inline editable document preview.
