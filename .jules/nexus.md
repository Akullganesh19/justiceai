## 2024-05-24 — Saved Documents
**Product understood as:** An AI-powered legal co-pilot that helps Indian citizens generate procedural legal documents and understand their rights.
**Derivation reasoning:** This product has user-generated legal documents. Users generate these documents repeatedly but the app never learns or remembers them (Pattern 3). Therefore users obviously need a way to save, view, and manage their generated documents — because legal processes are long-term and users need to reference or re-export previously generated notices. It doesn't exist because the focus was likely on the generation mechanism itself rather than post-generation lifecycle. I'm building it because it significantly reduces friction for users who need to return to previously drafted documents without re-entering all form data.
**Feature built:** Added persistent local storage for generated documents in the Procedural Drafting section, complete with a dedicated 'Saved Documents' UI for viewing, deleting, and re-exporting.
**User impact:** Users can now generate a document once and it will automatically be saved to their local browser storage, allowing them to retrieve, view, copy, or export it as a PDF at any point in the future.
**Next logical feature:** Since users can now save documents, the next logical step is to allow them to "Edit" or "Duplicate" a saved document by re-populating the `FormWizard` with the saved `formData`.
