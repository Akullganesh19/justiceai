## 2024-05-28 — Saved Documents Vault
**Product understood as:** An AI-powered legal co-pilot that helps citizens understand rights and generate legal documents.
**Derivation reasoning:** This product generates documents based on user input. Users generate complex documents (Consumer Petitions, RTI Applications, FIR Drafts). Therefore users obviously need a way to save and retrieve their generated documents, because currently if they navigate away they lose their generated work. It doesn't exist because the MVP focused on generation capability. I'm building it because users need to refer back to their drafts for editing or reference without retyping all information.
**Feature built:** Added persistent local storage for generated documents in the DocumentsPage, with a "Saved Documents" vault to view, retrieve, and delete past generations.
**User impact:** Users can now safely navigate away from the Documents page and return later to find their previously drafted documents still accessible.
**Next logical feature:** User activity/history dashboard aggregating past chats, saved documents, and tracked cases into one cohesive timeline.
