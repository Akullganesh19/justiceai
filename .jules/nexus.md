## 2024-07-18 — Document Vault
**Product understood as:** An AI legal co-pilot that helps Indian citizens understand rights and draft procedural legal documents.
**Derivation reasoning:** Users generate complex legal documents (like RTIs, FIRs, and Notices), but once they navigate away from the Documents page, the document is lost forever. This matches Pattern 3 (Actions Without Memory): users do X repeatedly but the app never remembers. Therefore, they obviously need a "Document Vault" to save and retrieve their previously generated documents.
**Feature built:** A Document Vault on the Documents Page that automatically saves generated documents to local storage, allowing users to view, copy, copy to clipboard, and re-download them as PDFs later.
**User impact:** Users can now safely generate multiple documents, leave the app, and return later to access their drafting history without needing to fill out the form again.
**Next logical feature:** Add a global search function across all history (chats, tracked cases, and documents) so users can quickly find past interactions and generated materials.
