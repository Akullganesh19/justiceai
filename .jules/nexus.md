## 2024-05-19 — AI Case Tracker Integration
**Product understood as:** An AI-powered legal copilot that helps citizens understand their rights, generate legal documents, and plan case strategies.
**Derivation reasoning:** The app generates a strategic timeline for a case in the chat analysis panel, and it also has a standalone Case Tracker where users manually create and track case milestones. Currently, these two systems are disconnected. Users receive a great AI-generated plan but have no way to operationalize it except by manually recreating it in the Case Tracker. Connecting these features is the logical next step.
**Feature built:** Added a "Track Case" action directly in the chat's AnalysisPanel (Timeline section) that automatically creates a new case in the Case Tracker populated with the AI-generated timeline steps.
**User impact:** Users can now instantly turn an AI consultation into an actionable, trackable case with one click, bridging the gap between advice and execution.
**Next logical feature:** Automated document drafting directly from specific timeline steps in the Case Tracker.
