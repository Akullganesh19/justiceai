## 2024-05-18 — Upcoming Hearings Widget
**Product understood as:** An AI-powered legal co-pilot that helps citizens understand their rights, generate documents, track legal cases, and get strategic advice based on Indian law.
**Derivation reasoning:** The application stores case details, including nextHearing dates in the Case Tracker, but fails to surface this critical information on the central Dashboard. It stores data without insight, meaning users have to manually visit the tracker to see if they have upcoming dates. Users obviously need a widget on the Dashboard showing Upcoming Deadlines or Next Hearings to stay on top of their case.
**Feature built:** "Upcoming Deadlines" widget on the DashboardPage.
**User impact:** Users are immediately notified of their most urgent impending case deadlines right when they log in to the system, reducing the risk of missing court dates.
**Next logical feature:** Context-aware document generation (passing Chat details directly into the Document Generator to auto-fill templates).
