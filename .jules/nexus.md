## 2024-05-18 — Saved Documents
**Product understood as:** An AI-powered legal co-pilot that helps Indian citizens understand rights and generate legal documents.
**Derivation reasoning:** The app generates complex legal documents like statutory notices, but users lose them upon refreshing. They invest time filling out forms but have no way to retrieve past work.
**Feature built:** Saved Documents repository using localStorage (`justice_ai_documents`), allowing users to save, view, and delete previously generated documents.
**User impact:** Users can now generate documents with confidence, knowing their work is safely stored locally and accessible later without starting over.
**Next logical feature:** Pre-filling document forms using identity data (saved user details) to eliminate repetitive typing across multiple documents.
