## 2024-10-25 — Document Vault
**Product understood as:** A comprehensive legal co-pilot helping ordinary Indian citizens understand their rights, draft procedural documents, and strategize legal cases.
**Derivation reasoning:** The product allows users to generate long procedural documents (like statutory notices) but the text only exists temporarily in the browser state; if the user navigates away, the drafted document is lost, which is a critical missing feature in a drafting workflow.
**Feature built:** A 'Document Vault' system that persists generated legal documents into localStorage and displays them for easy re-access or deletion on the Documents Page.
**User impact:** Users can now safely draft legal documents without fear of losing their progress if they navigate away, and they can retrieve old drafts to refer to or export at a later date.
**Next logical feature:** Integrating the saved documents directly into the case workspace (chat) so the AI can read and critique the user's drafted notices.
