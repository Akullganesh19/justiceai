## 2024-05-24 — Document Archive (Saved Documents)
**Product understood as:** An AI-powered legal co-pilot that helps citizens understand rights and draft procedural documents like legal notices and consumer complaints.
**Derivation reasoning:** This product has a drafting tool where users configure and download generated legal documents. Users currently have no way to view, revisit, or manage documents they previously drafted without starting over. Therefore, they obviously need a "Saved Documents" archive to review past drafts, modify them for new cases, and maintain a historical record.
**Feature built:** Added an auto-saving mechanism and an interactive 'DOCUMENT_ARCHIVE' section on the Documents page to persist generated documents in localStorage, allowing users to view, manage, and delete their previously generated drafts.
**User impact:** Users can now treat the document generator as a persistent legal repository, safely retaining generated notices and complaints without needing to immediately download them or worry about losing their work.
**Next logical feature:** A document editing interface allowing users to refine the AI-generated draft before saving or exporting it.
