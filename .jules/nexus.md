## 2024-05-24 — Saved Advocates (Bookmarking)
**Product understood as:** An AI-powered legal co-pilot that helps Indian citizens understand their rights, draft legal documents, track cases, and find lawyers.
**Derivation reasoning:** This product has a directory of verified lawyers with their fees, ratings, and experience. Users search for lawyers, filter them, and compare their profiles. Therefore users obviously need a way to save or bookmark specific lawyers — because choosing legal counsel is a high-stakes decision that is rarely made in a single session and often requires consulting with family. It doesn't exist because the initial implementation focused on search and filtering capabilities rather than user retention and workflow continuity. I'm building it because it significantly reduces friction for users who need to shortlist candidates and return later to contact them.
**Feature built:** Added bookmarking functionality to the Lawyer Finder page, allowing users to save lawyers to their local storage and filter the directory to show only their saved shortlists.
**User impact:** Users can now curate a personalized shortlist of advocates, making it easier to compare their options and contact them without having to search again.
**Next logical feature:** "Case History Export" — users can chat about their case but might need to share the chat history or AI's analysis with a real lawyer or family member.
