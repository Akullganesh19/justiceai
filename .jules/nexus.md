## 2024-07-26 — Document Vault
**Product understood as:** An AI-powered legal co-pilot that helps citizens understand their rights, navigate cases, and systematically generate statutory notices and procedural applications.
**Derivation reasoning:** Pattern 3: Actions Without Memory. Users are systematically generating procedural applications and legal notices via the Draft Protocol. However, once generated, they can only download or copy the document. If they close the browser or need to access a previously generated draft later, the app has forgotten it completely, forcing them to re-enter all fields. Therefore, users obviously need a Document Vault to save and retrieve previously generated documents locally.
**Feature built:** Added a "Document Vault" local storage system integrated into the Procedural Drafting screen. Users can now save generated documents directly from the Document Preview screen, and view their history of saved documents right below the template grid. They can also view old records or delete them.
**User impact:** Users can now generate, save, and recall multiple complex legal documents across sessions without losing their work.
**Next logical feature:** "Case to Document Sync" — users shouldn't have to type in case details to generate a document if they already have an active Case Tracker entry. The app could auto-fill the forms using the tracked case data.
