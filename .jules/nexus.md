## 2026-08-29 — Document Vault
**Product understood as:** An AI-powered legal co-pilot that includes generating specialized legal documents from templates.
**Derivation reasoning:** This product has document templates and users generate complex legal documents using forms. Therefore users obviously need a Document Vault — because generating a document takes effort (filling forms, configuring), and currently, if a user navigates away, the document is lost forever unless they downloaded it immediately. They need a place to securely save, view, and manage previously generated documents locally.
**Feature built:** Added a "Save to Vault" capability inside the Document Preview, and introduced a new UI section on the Documents page to browse, load, or delete saved documents from localStorage.
**User impact:** Users can now safely navigate away from their drafted legal documents and return to them later without having to refill long forms or regenerate the content.
**Next logical feature:** Ability to automatically synchronize Vault documents with a particular case in the Case Tracker so they don't exist in isolation.
