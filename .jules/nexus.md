## 2024-08-14 — Document Vault
**Product understood as:** A comprehensive AI-powered legal co-pilot that assists Indian citizens in generating procedural drafting applications and managing case data.
**Derivation reasoning:** The product currently generates procedural drafts (like FIRs or RTI requests) using existing templates and user inputs. Users go through a multi-step form to generate a document. However, once generated, if the user navigates away, the generated document is permanently lost because it is not saved anywhere. Users repeatedly generate documents but the app never learns or remembers their drafts. This is a classic "Actions Without Memory" gap. Given the user's intent to draft, review, and perhaps print or refer back to these legal documents later, they obviously need a way to save and revisit them without re-entering all the data.
**Feature built:** The "Document Vault"—a local storage-backed system within the `DocumentsPage` that allows users to save generated documents directly from the preview screen. It includes a dedicated Vault View where users can browse, view, and delete previously saved documents.
**User impact:** Users can now safely save their drafted legal documents, exit the flow, and return later to access or export them, significantly improving the utility and peace of mind when working on important procedural paperwork.
**Next logical feature:** Since users can now save drafted documents and they also have a Case Tracker, the next logical step is to associate these saved drafts directly with a specific tracked case, allowing a holistic view of the case history and its associated paperwork.
