## 2024-05-24 — Document Vault
**Product understood as:** An AI legal co-pilot that helps citizens understand their rights and generate procedural legal documents (notices, complaints, etc.).
**Derivation reasoning:** The app has a `DocumentGeneratorPage` and `DocumentsPage` where users spend time filling out structured forms to generate complex legal documents. However, after generation, there is no system memory. Users must immediately copy or download the document, or it is lost forever if they navigate away. Users generate documents (action). The app has `localStorage` for Chat and Case Tracker (existing data patterns). Therefore users obviously need a Document Vault — because generating a legal document is an iterative process requiring drafts and history. It doesn't exist because the generator was built as a stateless utility. I'm building it because users need a safe place to find their past drafts without recreating them.
**Feature built:** Added a "Document Vault" to `DocumentsPage.jsx` using `localStorage.getItem('justice_ai_documents')`. Users can now save generated documents directly from the preview screen, view a gallery of their saved drafts, and restore/delete them.
**User impact:** Users can safely draft documents, leave the page, and return later to retrieve their previous work without losing progress.
**Next logical feature:** Bulk export or sharing of saved documents.
