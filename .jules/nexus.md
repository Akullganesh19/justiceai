## 2024-05-24 — Saved Drafts History
**Product understood as:** An AI legal co-pilot that helps citizens understand rights and draft legal documents.
**Derivation reasoning:** This product has Document Generators. Users generate legal notices, complaints, RTI applications, FIRs. Therefore users obviously need a history of their generated drafts — because right now, if they navigate away from the page, their draft is lost forever.
**Feature built:** Saved Drafts History in DocumentsPage, storing generated documents in localStorage.
**User impact:** Users can now access, review, copy, and re-download their past generated legal documents without re-entering all the information.
**Next logical feature:** User profiles or cross-device syncing for history.
